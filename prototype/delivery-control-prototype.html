Delivery Control Prototype

Call to Granola, RAID, requirement pack, clarity questions, Pulse tickets, validation, Git branch.
Prototype with simulated data. Granola, Slack, Pulse and GitHub are placeholders: nothing is read from or sent to any of them, and the "created" tickets and branches below do not exist. The checks on quotes, acceptance criteria, owners, dates and ticket quality run for real, so you can test the rules. AI drafting is simulated with sample text.
How I read your workflow, and what to try
Call with client, recorded on Granola
Step 1 (you paste the transcript; a summary is refused)
Questions asked, scope clear, RAID
Step 2 (R/A/I/D table, each item tied to a quote)
RAID is the input to the requirement pack
Step 3 (pack drafted from transcript + RAID, checked live)
System asks questions based on clarity from pack and recording
Step 4 (clarity meter, questions to Slack, answers)
Clear, then Pulse: epic, story or task, risk or dependency (task + label)
Step 5 (needs Pulse key and the feature-doc ID)
Delivery manager validates before development; tech lead optional
Step 6 (hard stop until checks pass; the tech lead may also validate)
Tech lead sizes, optional, any time before development
Optional step at the bottom of the list (closes when development starts)
Git repo is the input at development; GitHub flow branching
Step 7 (needs repo and base branch; branch must name a created story ticket)

Things to try:

  • Click "Fill sample inputs", then work down the steps. Each locked step tells you which input it is waiting for.
  • Paste an AI summary (bullets and headings) into step 1: it is refused. Paste fewer than 100 words: refused.
  • Replace the transcript with a long, vague paragraph (100 or more words, no volumes, dates or boundaries): step 3 locks, no model is used, and the delivery manager must give a reason to proceed anyway.
  • Empty the transcript: step 3 locks. Clear the Slack channel: step 4 cannot post questions.
  • In step 3, edit a [Q: "..."] quote to words nobody said, or add a scope item with no quote. It is blocked.
  • In step 4, leave a question unanswered. Step 5 stays locked. Try "Treat as assumption".
  • In step 6, validate as the tech lead only: it is refused until the delivery manager validates. Tick "tech lead must also validate" to see sizing become a requirement.
  • Open the optional Sizing step. Pick the delivery manager as the person entering sizes and save: refused. Switch to the tech lead and save. After you share a branch in step 7, sizing closes.
  • In step 7, edit the branch name: try feature/returns-export, main or another project's key. Each is refused with the reason.
  • Create the tickets in step 5, then open the optional Change request step: the pack reopens, validation must be redone, and step 5 shows which tickets are new, changed or removed. Also try adding a follow-up call in step 1.
  • Open "Model per step" under the step panel. Change a tier and watch the estimate move. Then break the pack in step 3 and press "Draft pack" twice: the redraft escalates to a stronger model after the number of failed checks you set.
  • In step 5, open a ticket's description: each carries the client's words and the answers, so it stands alone.
